    Types of Icebreaking

    Preventative Icebreaking (PI) - Domestic icebreaking conducted in advance of unspecified commercial vessel movements. Includes channel/track maintenance, track grooming, and flushing operations.

    Vessel Assistance (VA) - Domestic icebreaking conducted to assist the general movement of commercial vessel traffic. A cutter's track work which facilitates the movement of a vessel(s) within 24 hours of the effort, the time to transit to a beset vessel, or a specified vessel requiring assistance.

    Direct Assistance (DA) - Domestic icebreaking conducted to assist a specific benefitting vessel(s). An icebreaker’s effort to free a beset vessel or conduct an escort when the assisted vessel(s) would be unable to transit without the support of an icebreaker.

    Miscellaneous (MC) - Domestic icebreaking hours not associated with the specific operations above. Generally, these hours include all other time spent underway in support of ice operations.
